# 🌾 KrishiLink AI (कृषि लिंक)

**KrishiLink AI** is an AI-powered digital platform designed to help farmers get better access to markets, understand crop prices, and connect with buyers.

The idea is simple: **make agricultural market information easier to understand and make selling crops more transparent.**

KrishiLink AI follows a **farmer-first approach**, with a simple interface and voice-based interaction to make the platform easier to use, especially for farmers who may not be comfortable with complex apps or typing.

## 🚀 Live Demo

🌐 **Live Website:** https://krishilink-ai.vercel.app

## ✨ What can KrishiLink AI do?

### 🌾 Farmer Marketplace
Farmers can list their crops and provide details such as quantity, expected price, and location.

### 💰 Price Discovery
Helps farmers understand available market prices so they can make better decisions before selling their produce.

### 🤝 Buyer–Farmer Connection
Buyers can discover available crop lots and interact with farmers through offers and negotiations.

### 🔄 Offer & Negotiation System
Farmers and buyers can make, accept, reject, or counter offers instead of relying only on fixed prices.

### 🎤 AI Voice Assistant
Farmers can ask questions using their voice and receive AI-powered responses, making the platform easier to use for users who may have difficulty typing or navigating traditional applications.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Assistance
The AI service provides agricultural and market-related guidance based on the information available to the platform.

### 📊 Market Insights
Provides useful information about commodities, markets, prices, and trading activity to support better decision-making.

### 🔐 Secure Access
Authentication and role-based access control help separate the capabilities of farmers, buyers, and other users.

## 🎯 Why KrishiLink AI?

Farmers often face difficulties in finding reliable market information, comparing prices, and connecting directly with suitable buyers.

KrishiLink AI aims to bring these activities into **one simple platform** while using AI and voice interaction to make technology more accessible.

The goal isn't to replace existing agricultural markets. It's to give farmers **better information, better connections, and more control when making selling decisions.**
